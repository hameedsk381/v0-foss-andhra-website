## Executive Summary

FOSS Andhra (`fossap.in`) is the primary digital presence for the Free & Open Source Software community in Andhra Pradesh. The site was built with modern technology (Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS) and has a well-organized structure across 17 pages and 7 program sections. The visual design is professional and the mission is clearly communicated.

However, the site's SEO performance is critically compromised by five fundamental omissions that, once corrected, could realistically improve organic visibility by 10–50× within 60–90 days:

1. **A single typo in robots.txt** (pointing to `fossandhra.org` instead of `fossap.in`) has caused Google to only index 1 of 17 pages. This is the #1 priority fix — it takes 5 minutes.

2. **No meta descriptions exist** on any of the 17+ pages, meaning Google auto-generates SERP snippets with no brand control.

3. **Zero structured data** — no Organization schema, no FAQ schema, no Event schema. The site is invisible to AI-powered search (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT).

4. **Blog and Events pages are empty shells** in static HTML because content loads via JavaScript after page render, making all blog posts and events completely invisible to search engines.

5. **No social sharing metadata** (OG tags) means the site shows blank previews on every social platform, killing virality from a community that likely shares heavily on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X.

The good news: fixes 1–3 combined require about 3 hours of development work and could unlock indexation of 16 currently invisible pages.

## Top 5 Critical Issues

### 1. Sitemap Domain Mismatch — Blocks 16/17 Pages from Indexing
`public/robots.txt` contains: `Sitemap: https://fossandhra.org/sitemap.xml`

`fossandhra.org` is a dead domain (DNS NXDOMAIN — confirmed). The sitemap at `fossap.in/sitemap.xml` also lists all 17 URLs under the `fossandhra.org` domain, not `fossap.in`.

**Result:** Google has indexed approximately 1 page (the homepage was found via direct crawl). All remaining 16 pages — including all 7 program pages, about, events, blog, FAQ, contact, contribute, membership, and legal pages — are not appearing in search.

**Fix time: 5 minutes** (change one URL in `public/robots.txt`)

---

### 2. Zero Meta Descriptions Site-Wide
17+ pages. Zero meta descriptions. Every SERP snippet is auto-generated by Google.

Meta descriptions don't directly affect rankings but strongly affect click-through rate (CTR). Missing descriptions typically reduce CTR by 5–15% compared to well-crafted ones.

**Fix time: 2 hours** (write + implement across all pages)

---

### 3. No Structured Data Anywhere
No JSON-LD detected on any page. This means:
- No Organization/NGO entity in Google's Knowledge Graph
- No FAQ rich results (for AI citation purposes)
- No Event markup for local event searches
- No Article schema for blog posts
- No BreadcrumbList for SERP navigation

**Fix time: 30 minutes** for Organization schema; 1 hour for full implementation

---

### 4. Blog & Events Content Not Crawlable
Both pages load content via client-side JavaScript. Search engines see blank pages. This eliminates:
- All blog post ranking opportunities (topic authority, long-tail keywords)
- All event-related searches ("FOSS workshop Vijayawada", "open source events AP 2026")

**Fix time: 4–8 hours** (convert to Next.js Server Components or ISR)

---

### 5. No Canonical URLs, No OG Tags
No `<link rel="canonical">` on any page (duplicate content risk).
No `og:image`, `og:title`, `og:description` on any page (blank social previews).

**Fix time: 1 hour** combined
